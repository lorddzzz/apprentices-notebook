## 2. Proposals (decisions the design leaves open)

- **FT-D1 Playback logic is its own first pack (FT Pack 0), test-first.** One pure projection `projectBatch(start, events, knowledge, scale)` over the **emitted engine batches** (the existing `buildSteps` stays the timing layer under it; its signature stays compatible, new fields only) feeds markers, readout, field notes (log) and gold firsts. No second projection anywhere. `beatOf(event, knowledge)` classifies each shown step `ordinary | burst | compound | first`. Controller exposes `readout`, `markers`, `dimmed`, `speed`. Starts from the existing projection tests (`tests/app/playback.test.ts`).
- **FT-D2 What counts as a "first" (changed after review).** Two kinds, never mixed: a **named discovery** (a `named` event, compound/burst/idiom, not in `profile.named` / `flow.newDiscoveries`) and a **witnessed line** (a reaction **line** id not in the session's `witnessed` set). `BattleSession.dispatch` appends the whole batch and calls `onEvents` before playing it, so reading the preview context afterwards would erase every first. Therefore **both sets are snapshotted before dispatch appends** (`KnowledgeSnapshot { named: ReadonlySet, witnessed: ReadonlySet | null }` captured in `dispatch`), the projection runs on (snapshot, batch), and within the batch a first is **de-duplicated** (one gold mark per id, attached to the first visible cause that carries it; several events inheriting one line yield one first). `witnessed == null` (Lab) → no firsts. The existing discovery toast moves to the projection's timing owner (FT Pack 0 owns `battle.svelte.ts` for this): it announces **when its step is shown, never earlier**, so it cannot reveal the result. No engine or save change.
- **FT-D3 Step numbering and source index (changed after review).** Only shown steps are numbered (`ms > 0`; silent `trace`/`named` and 0 ms events fold into the next shown step, as `Sequencer` does). Each shown step carries `{ n, srcIndex (log index of its cause), actionId (boundary: numbering restarts per player/enemy action; `n` is per action), text, anchor?: Coord | null, first?: FirstMark[] }`. Rules: an event with no board cell (phase change, win) becomes a **readout-only** step (no marker); two steps on one cell stack as one marker group (offset, shared-cell collision test); a multi-cell burst gets **one anchor** (its origin cell). The pocket Log groups by action then round and shows the same numbers; two actions in one round do not renumber each other; **Skip leaves a complete log without replaying it** (the projection result is appended in full, markers cleared); a resumed fight shows its log without markers. The log is derived from the session event log, never persisted.
- **FT-D4 Tap = faster, Skip = result (changed after review).** Tap = ×2, a second tap ×4 (capped); Skip = result. Both stay (player decides at UX-G2 whether ×4 is needed). Single timing table (all values at scale 1 = Motion Normal; Quick halves via `motionScale()`):

  | Moment | ×1 | ×2 | ×4 | Skip |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Ordinary step | `STEP_MS` (160/260/420/500) | half | quarter | jumps to the final view, FX cleared |
  | Held beat (`--dur-held`) | 800 ms | 250 ms (fixed, not 800/2) | 100 ms | dropped |
  | Held beat, reduced motion | 300 ms cross-fade | 300 ms cross-fade (speed does not shorten a cross-fade) | same | dropped |

  Mid-hold: the remaining time becomes `min(remaining, table value)` at the new rate; a rate change never lengthens anything. Speed resets to ×1 at the next action (`dispatch`). **Busy-input table (every input and timer):** during playback, board and readout taps → `speedUp()` (not hit-cell activation, not caption skip); member select, spell, Undo, confirm and End turn are **inert** (no queued action, no speed-up; `swallow()` keeps its current return-early behaviour for them, the speed-up is routed only from board/readout handlers); Skip is the only jump-to-result; the caption no longer skips. A press/hold in progress when playback ends is released without activating (existing release suppression kept; tested with a pointer held across the final step). During the enemy turn: same table (the enemy turn is playback). During the end overlay: board taps inert, only the overlay's controls act. Reduced motion (OS flag) and Quick (device pref) are **independent**: reduced-motion removes trails/slides/flourish and makes the hold a cross-fade; Quick scales time only; both together = cross-fade at half time. Natural completion, skip, replace (new dispatch) and dispose each resolve `onDone` exactly once (fake clock with an elapsed-time source). `FxLayer`'s own float timers and CSS animations are driven from the same scale and are cleared at rest (rendered-FX-clear test).
- **FT-D5 One held beat per cause (changed after review).** 0.8 s = `--dur-held`; bursts, compounds and firsts only; if one cause carries several (a burst that is also a first) it still holds once and shows one card with the gold ring; board scrim 30% over layers 1–3 (never over the overlay or markers), name card, gold ring for firsts. Rate precedence: speed table (FT-D4) > Motion scale > default. Reduced motion: markers cross-fade, no flourish, no trails, hold = 0.3 s cross-fade card.
- **FT-D6 Motion pref arrives from UF (R5), with a named owner (changed after review).** FT Pack 0 reads `motionScale()` from `src/app/timing.ts` (a reduced-motion-only stand-in) until UF-11 merges; **FT-3b (Pack 0) is the named hookup task**: when UF-11 is on main the orchestrator dispatches it (Owed list), it switches the import to `prefs.svelte.ts` and re-runs the speed/reduced-motion matrix. Speed (FT-D4) and motion suppression stay separate inputs.
- **FT-D7 District fight art is convention-based, with one registration path (program R1, R2; changed after review).** Ids (UA's C2 files; the LayerId→kebab map is UA-1's style sheet §7): scene `scenes/<district>/fight` (+ `arena`), tile `tiles/<district|shared>/<material>[-<state>]`, figure `units/shared/<def>` or `foes/<district>/<def>`. Crests are vector, not art ids. `ArtImage` fallback is today's vector; inside the board SVG the same art is drawn with `<image href={artUrl(id)}>` when `artUrl` is non-null, else the vector (a test asserts the rendered `<image>` hrefs for a fixture, not just "asset exists"). **Subject and lookup order (changed after review):** art is keyed by the **owning definition**, even when a foe or prop is borrowed (a `deadLetter` on a drawn page resolves `foes/inkQuarter/dead-letter`); lookup = the owning district's id → shared material → vector. District/arena **context** is passed explicitly: `Combat.svelte` → `BattleView` (new optional prop `district?: DistrictId`, `arena?: boolean`) → `BattleBoard` (`kit`). Defaults for callers without it: Lab and Gallery pass the district of the sample (or none → `shared` art + vector); UT's read-only board passes the district of the wiped trip's room; none = no scene, shared tiles, vector figures. Shared-material and floor variants resolve `tiles/<district|shared>/<material>[-<state>]`; stateful district art (cracked span, struck bell) keeps its state suffix. Underjoin and drawn-page scenes/floors use what UA-25/UA-26 actually commission (Underjoin floor/scene ids, `scenes/inkedPage/fight`), with the Seam tint as fallback only when a file is absent. **`DistrictFightTheme` is defined by FT-4 in `components/board/fightArt.ts`**: `{ id: DistrictId; scene?: ArtId; sceneFocal?: [number, number]; arena?: ArtId; tiles?: Partial<Record<TileKind, ArtId>>; tileTone?: …; crest?: Component<{ size: number }> }`, and FT-4 adds the optional `fightTheme?: DistrictFightTheme` field to `DistrictUi` in `src/ui/districts/index.ts` (additive, R1) plus `crestFor(id)` (disc fallback). `FIGHT_KITS` in `fightArt.ts` is the lookup over `DISTRICT_UI[id].fightTheme`, **not a second registry**. Seam and Lampwater entries are FT-12 (in `districts/seam/index.ts`, `canals/index.ts`); the other districts fill their own `fightTheme` in UD-5, UD-7, UD-9, UD-11, UD-13, UD-14, UD-15.
- **FT-D8 Two kinds of oversized and rival figure (R13, changed after review).** Engine units occupy one cell. **True bosses** (the two with `boss: true` in `src/data/enemies.ts` that are not rivals: e.g. the heart bosses) use the **192×256 canvas** (feet y = 232), drawn at figure scale (cell/128), 1.5 cells wide and 2 tall, anchored to their one cell, overlapping neighbours by art only, with their own cell outlined by the overlay. **Rivals** (the shells in `RIVALS`, including those also flagged `boss: true`, and Lamplighter, which needs a rival header independently of that flag) use the **normal 128×176 canvas**; the header kind comes from "is a rival" (`RIVALS` membership), never from `boss`. **Mother and marginalia** (lent-heart large units) are sized as large art too. Art ids bind to real `ENEMY_IDS` through the id mapping in `src/ui/art/` (UF-2/UA, Consumes: `enemyArtId(def)`; the kebab-case mapping is not rebuilt here). Dimensions are keyed by asset id (`figureSize(assetId)`), and the board reserves headroom at the top edge for a 2-cell-tall boss. Tests: one true boss, a boss-flagged rival and a plain rival; arena selection; the legal-cell outline; a neighbouring target tappable under the painting. Rival-sheet content stays within the existing inspection rules.
- **FT-D9 Fight layout budget (replaces the old board sizing; changed after review; program R20).** At 375×812 (734 px after 44/34 safe areas) a 7×9 board keeps **≥ 44 px cells**. ## 3. Current implementation and risks

- `Combat.svelte` builds `BattleView` with `TopBar`; `BattleView.svelte` stacks DistrictBanners, TrickBanner, BossHeader, board, Caption, PartyStrip, ActionBar, Footer; `Lab.svelte` mounts the same `BattleView` with its own `top` snippet (keep that prop; the Lab restyle is UE's).

- Playback: `buildSteps` (one `Step` per non-silent event: view, fx, ms), `Sequencer` (injected clock, skips 0 ms steps, `skip()` once), `Playback` ($state wrapper, `prefersReducedMotion` → scale 0.5), `durationOf` in `timing.ts` (`STEP_MS` 160/260/420/500), `BattleSession.busy/view/fx/fxKey`, `overlay` empty while busy. Tests: `tests/app/playback.test.ts` (fake clock), `fx.test.ts`, `tests/ui/geometry.test.ts`, `fixtures.test.ts`.
- Fixtures: `galleryFixtures.ts` (`ROOM_SAMPLES` includes 7×9, `OIL_CHAIN_*`), `tests/fixtures/saves/*-mid-trip.json`, `tests/app/battleFixtures.ts`.
- **Risks, riskiest first**: (1) held-beat timing and tap-faster inside `Sequencer` (rate change mid-timer, skip during a hold) → FT Pack 0 first. (2) Five layers plus 3/4 figures lapping rows and keeping hit rects ≥44 px on 7×9 (FT-5, FT-9). (3) Performance of raster tiles + SVG filters on an iPhone (use one `<image>` per tile, no filters; shadow baked into the figure art; measure in Close). (4) Whiteboard's "2×2 boss" vs one-cell engine units (FT-D8). (5) Fixtures frozen mid-chain need a dev-only `Playback.freezeAt(i)` keyed by the **shown ordinal** (FT-3). (7) Fight space: the budget (FT-D9) is tight at 7×9; the layout spike FT-5s runs before A2. (6) Districts' `unitArt`/`boardLayers`/`banners` (`ui/districts/index.ts`) still draw in old vector style: they must keep working untouched; UD restyles them.
