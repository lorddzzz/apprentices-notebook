# Actions and habits: Pull, Hop, Swap and the seven new habits (AH)

> Design: [party.md](../design/party.md) §1–§2 (§0d wins over §0b/§0c), [rune rules](../design/rune-design-final.md) §3, §4.4, §7, §9. Roadmap: **BL-130** (part of BL-43). Brief: [build-program.md](build-program.md).
> **State: Drafted (not approved).**
> Scope: the basic actions Pull, Hop and Swap (Shove exists) with landing previews and arrivals through FD's pipeline, and the seven new habits as generic, data-keyed rules that any apprentice, the Lab or a rival can carry, each previewed, with witnessed fine print and a scenario test.

## 1. Scope and settled rules

**Delivers**
- **Pull, Hop, Swap** as player actions on the uncharged cast slot, no ink, always available to an apprentice whose `MemberUnit.action` names them (party.md §1 "Basic actions"). Each shows its landing in the preview, lands through FD's `arrive`/`relocateAll`, and has the edge-case rules party.md §3 leaves to the build (§2 below).
- **Seven habits**, keyed by habit id (not by apprentice): the runaway's Fuse-on-step (body), the notary's double mark for Tally, the towel-bearer's steamproofing (body) with the **boil-over hook** BH will call, the warden's second Hold, the tuner's near Unison, the Copyist's long Touch, the erased student's restoring Erase (party.md §2 table).
- Habit guidelines from party.md §1: a lattice habit bends one headline only on her own page and casts; a body habit is one rule about her body and never covers the others; no habit adds damage, ink or a free cast; each is **shown in the preview**, has **witnessed fine print** and ships with a **scenario test**; the same hook serves as a rival's one trick.
- **Lab kits:** the Lab and the scenario harness can give any fielded apprentice any action and habit, so every mechanic is playable before RC adds a single recruit.

**Leaves out** (owner): the seven apprentices' data entries, preconditions, offers and codex presence (RC); the registry, `MemberUnit.action` and the shove gate (HC); the arrival pipeline, cause registry and relocation event shape (FD); the boil-over gauge itself (BH); rival foes that carry a trick (RV); the Cartographers' un-draw of unfinished paper (CG/DK).

## 2. Proposals

**Actions** (all four take a `dir`; the tap picks the direction)

| # | Proposal | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | `Action` gains `{ type: 'pull' \| 'hop' \| 'swap'; unit; dir }`, shaped like `shove`. One engine registry `BASIC_ACTIONS: Record<BasicActionId, { dirs(s, unit), apply(ctx, unit, dir) }>` serves legality, dispatch, the input mode, the bot and RV's foes; Shove is registered by wrapping the existing `movement.ts::shove`. Any basic action clears earlier move undos, as cast and shove do | One table instead of a `switch` per call site; `shove` keeps its event and logs, so no scenario churn |
| P2 | **Legal vs fizzle:** geometry and permanent traits decide legality (the option is not offered, with a note); temporary states (Hold, a plate in the way) are legal and fizzle with a witnessed line, as Shove into a held unit does today | Mirrors Shove; fine print stays discoverable, the picker stays honest |
| P3 | **The reach rule** (Hop and long Touch): passing over one tile is allowed over a unit, jar, cloud, any lingering state, water, a drop or a cut, but **not over a tile that blocks units** (`queries.ts::blocksUnits`: plates of every kind, Walls, kettles, skid plates, lock gates, grates). **Redefines:** party.md's "cannot cross a wall" now also covers plates, gates and grates | One predicate the player already knows from walking; "wall" alone would make a brass plate low and a Wall tall, which the board does not show |
| P4 | **Pull:** legal on any living unit (ally or foe) exactly 2 tiles away in a straight line with **no unit on the middle tile** (the hook would catch that one, and it is adjacent already). Not legal on anchored foes. The target is pushed 1 tile toward the caster with **no crush**: a blocking middle tile, a held target or a swimmer that would leave water means it stays (`pull:plate`, `pull:held`, `pull:water`); a land unit pulled onto canal water or a drop falls by the drop rule (`pull:drop`). Fliers are pulled and never fall. The caster always stands next to the landing, so a pulled unit never slides further (party.md's "slides on oil" has nothing to slide into); entered tiles per FD P2. A Pull does not move the caster, so a shackled or held caster may Pull | Keeps every push rule except the crush; the drop is Shove's existing trick in reverse |
| P5 | **Hop:** legal to a tile exactly 2 away in a straight line that a walk could end on (no unit, not blocked, not water or a drop), over a middle tile allowed by P3. Landing on a jar, fire, a stain or a cloud is legal (§9 "hop onto your own jar to open it"). Spends the action, not the move; a held or shackled apprentice can't Hop. Sets `lastMove` to the hop direction (Touch-on-self follows it) | Hop is a move by herself, so the Shackle and Hold rules apply as they do to walking |
| P6 | **Swap (action):** legal with an adjacent living unit when each could stand on the other's tile; never an anchored or flying foe (party.md §1), never a swimmer off water. A held caster or target fizzles with the existing `swap:held` line; a shackled caster can't Swap (she moves herself), a shackled target can. Arrivals run caster then target through `relocateAll` (FD P4). Sets `lastMove` to `dir`. The **Swap rune** keeps its own rules apart from FD's anchored handling | Puts a foe on the wet tile or an ally out of the telegraph; a land body never lands in a canal by accident |
| P7 | **Basic actions never count for Unison** and never earn footnote points (they are not casts or runes). Their lines use subjects `pull` and `hop`; Swap reuses `swap:*`, Shove keeps `shove:*` | Unison reads "another apprentice's cast"; footnotes are for runes |
| P8 | **Telegraphs follow the foe** as with Shove today (a pulled or swapped foe attacks from its new tile in its telegraphed direction); the preview redraws intents from the previewed state (`overlay.ts` already does) | No new rule; moving telegraphs is the point of the actions |

**Habits**

| # | Proposal | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P9 | **Habit rules live in data:** `HABITS` (HC's `src/data/habits.ts`) gains a `rule` per habit with only the field it bends: `jarLimit`, `forkAhead`, `fuseOnStep`, `tallyMarkWeight`, `wards: HarmKind[]`, `holdExtra`, `unisonReach`, `touchReach`, `eraseRestores`. The engine asks `habitOf(unit)` (member: `lean.habit`; foe: `ENEMIES[def].trick ?? null`) and `habitRule(unit)`; flows keep `flow.habit`, now set from `habitOf(caster)` so a jar or repeat carries its owner's habit. `twoJars` and `forkAhead` move onto the table with no behaviour change | Habits stay keyed by id, numbers stay in `src/data/`, and a rival's trick is one data field (party.md §1 last bullet) |
| P10 | **Ids:** `fuseOnStep` (runaway), `markTwice` (notary), `steamproof` (towel-bearer), `holdTwo` (warden), `nearUnison` (tuner), `longTouch` (Copyist), `restoreErase` (erased student). `leansOn`: Fuse, Tally, none, Hold, Unison, Touch, Erase | RC keys the seven entries to these; `leansOn` drives HC's dormant flag and RC's preconditions |
| P11 | **Headline vs fine print:** the habit's headline effect is tagged with a new event field `habit` (like `line`), never masked in previews (the card states it, like a rune headline). Its **edge cases** are ordinary lines with the habit as subject, masked until witnessed (P41) and banked by `witnessedIn`. `LineSubject` grows by `HabitId \| 'pull' \| 'hop'` | Satisfies both "shown in the preview" and "fine print witnessed like a rune's" |
| P12 | **fuseOnStep:** her own Fuse (dusk) jars behave as step-jars for her only: an AH arrival hook in FD's habits list, answering exactly the tiles the core `stepJar` hook answers, for every cause (walk, pushed, pulled, hopped, swapped). Line `fuseOnStep:jar` ("…even when she is pushed or swapped onto it") | §0d's intent with one hook; consistent with how step-jars answer arrivals |
| P13 | **markTwice:** in her Tally, a mark on the target counts 2 (a deep mark is still one mark: 2, not 3; line `markTwice:deepMark`) | Literal reading of §2; the deep-mark case is the one surprise |
| P14 | **steamproof:** `dealDamage` takes an optional `harm: HarmKind`; a warded unit takes nothing, logs `unharmed` (habit-tagged), and it **is not a hit** (no `hit` hook, shield untouched). Tagged `steam`: Storm, Thunderhead, Brand storm, the Kettle's heat and the Kettle blowing, and BH's boil-over. **Flashover is fire** and still burns her (line `steamproof:greaseFog`); a Brand storm still marks her (line `steamproof:brandCloud`). **Hook for BH:** the boil-over calls `dealDamage(ctx, u, dmg, 'Boil-over', [], { …, harm: 'steam' })` (or the `storm` helper with `harm`), nothing else | "Steam never hurts her" without touching fire; one generic ward any later body habit or rival can reuse |
| P15 | **holdTwo:** each Hold she lands also holds the first other living unit adjacent to the target, scanning **ahead along the heading, then left, then right, then behind** (Fork's sides, R5; no heading: up, right, down, left), skipping herself and units this cast already held. Allies count (holding one protects it through the enemy phase; line `holdTwo:member`). Same rounds, gift included | Deterministic, previewable, and reads like Fork; no extra tap |
| P16 | **nearUnison:** her Unison also passes when another apprentice's cast this phase landed on a tile **orthogonally adjacent** to the target. Unit entries count where that unit stood in her cast's snapshot (R8; line `nearUnison:unit`) | "Beside" means the four neighbours everywhere else (Seep, Kettle) |
| P17 | **longTouch:** her Touch also aims the four tiles exactly 2 away in a straight line, over a middle tile allowed by P3 (lines `longTouch:cloud`, `longTouch:unit`: it reaches where Bolt would stop). Heading as today (`axisToward`) | A safer close caster without becoming a second Bolt |
| P18 | **restoreErase:** `BattleState.erased` records what the **last Erase that removed anything** took, per target (state with its rounds and `set`, jar, mark, collar, shackle, each with its origin), whoever cast it; it lasts the battle until a newer Erase replaces it. **Her** Erase on a target that holds nothing to erase, and that the record lists, puts that target's part back and consumes it; otherwise she erases as usual. A restored jar goes through `sealJar` (counts as its owner's newest, J4; line `restoreErase:jar`); a mark returns only to the unit that bore it (`restoreErase:mark`); a state that would stand under a unit stays gone (`restoreErase:unit`). Restored things keep their original origin (elegance) | Undo the others' setups (§2 "reading the roster"), deterministic, no new aim mode |

## 3. Current implementation and risks

- **Actions:** `movement.ts::shove`, `battle.ts::isLegal`/`dispatch`/`applyAction` (undo clearing names `cast`/`shove`), `run.ts` saved-action parser (line ~1193), `tripStats.ts`, `app/battleInput.ts` (`shove` mode, `onShove`, `tapShove`, `previewOf` path for shove), `overlay.ts` (`shove` highlight), `describe.ts`, `fx.ts`, `timing.ts`, `view.ts`, `ActionBar.svelte`, `BattleView.svelte`, `HighlightLayer.svelte`. `push.ts::push` always crushes; Pull needs a no-crush variant of the same planner.
- **Habits:** read inline: `leans.ts::jarLimit` (`twoJars`), `resolve.ts` fork branch (`forkAhead`); `flow.habit` is set member-only in `resolve.ts`, `jars.ts::runDetached`, `marks.ts`. Tally (`runes/flow.ts::tallyCount`) and Unison (`unisonWith`) read the snapshot; Touch aims come from `targets.ts` and `queries.ts::legalAims`; Hold and Erase are in `runes/actions.ts`; storm and kettle damage in `bursts.ts::storm` and `mix.ts` round-end. Built habits have **no fine print lines** today; `LineSubject` is runes, methods and solvents only.
- **Risks:** (1) everything rides on FD-3/FD-7; Pull/Hop/Swap without the pipeline would re-create the inconsistencies FD removes. (2) `LineSubject` growth touches footnote point counting and the line integrity test (non-rune subjects must earn no points). (3) `dealDamage` is called from ~30 places; only steam sources gain `harm`, and `lines.ts::hurtBy` must treat `unharmed` as no harm. (4) The input mode rename (`shove` → `basic`) touches UI tests and the Gallery. (5) A restore record is battle state: clone, projection and fuzz must carry it.
