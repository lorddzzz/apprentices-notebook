# The bathhouses: boil-over, rising heat and the steam's people (BH)

> Design: [district-bathhouses.md](../design/district-bathhouses.md), with [rune-design-final.md](../design/rune-design-final.md) §4 (clouds, Storm), §5.2 (Areas), §6.3 (Bath salt, Fog jar), §11 (Steam Room); [party.md](../design/party.md) §2 (the towel-bearer); [district-seam.md](../design/district-seam.md) (the chimney and the floor); [footnotes.md](../design/footnotes.md). Roadmap: **BL-114**. Prefix **BH**. Brief: [build-program.md](build-program.md).
> **State: Drafted (not approved).**
> Scope: the thermae as a district module built on the kit: the boil-over gauge, heat 1–4 with richer offers and the cold plunge, the dissolved bather, the attendant and the boilerman, five fights (four rooms and the heart), a stand-in Bath-Steward until RV's authoring API, the storm-on-3 hearing, the "fill the dome" threshold, the bather's-ledger lore, the towel-bearer's landmark room, the steam-vent floor of the lower Seam, the footnote batch, Bench parity and bot calibration.

## 1. Scope and settled rules

**Delivers**

- **Boil-over** as a round-end board rule with its own state, forecast and HUD meter (design §0 "Board rule", §1 "The gauge").
- **Heat** (1–4, one per room, the heart at 4), **richer offers as it climbs**, a second pick at heat 4, the **cold plunge** offer, **free return after any room** (design §0 "Trip shape", "What the heat gives").
- **The steam's people:** dissolved bather, attendant, boilerman, with the AI and telegraphs each needs.
- **Four rooms and the heart** (five fights, no landings), three layouts per depth, the towel-bearer's landmark room (room 3).
- **The Bath-Steward:** a scripted stand-in heart through DK-9, then the real rival through RV's API (RV-11–14, after the Lab gate) with his page.
- The **storm-on-3 hearing**, the **"fill the dome"** threshold, the **bather's ledger** lore, the district's map caption and line.
- The **lower-Seam floor intrusion** (a steam vent) for SM.
- The **footnote batch** (FN §6): Vat, Seep, Steep, Bath salt, Fog jar, the Steward's note, Steep's non-diagonal capstone.
- **Bench parity** registrations and **bot calibration**; the district flips from `drawing` to open only after the playtest gate.

**Settled by design (cited, not repeated):** Storm result = 2 to every unit (§4.3); the school and its solvents (§11); a boil-over storms everyone in a cloud, party included (district §0); the cold plunge costs a pick and cools the next room by one, never below 1; free return; the Steward steams away and cutting the cloud network is the answer; the hearing is a Spark through a cloud hitting 3+ units in the Seam; the idiom Steam Room is the signature (district §1); the towel-bearer is a loot offer at a landmark room and the boil-over skips her (party §2).

**Leaves out:** the recruit offer's semantics and hidden-until-ready rules (RC), the towel-bearer's body, Pull and the damage-immunity hook (AH), the rival engine and the trick API (RV), the district kit itself (DK), the lower-Seam floor framework (SM), the arrival pipeline (FD), rooms' size rules (HC), the footnote system (FN), real names and terrain art (BL-40).

## 2. Proposals

| # | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Heat is a four-row table read by the gauge, not a vent schedule.** `HEAT[h] = { tileQuarters, ventQuarters }`; the room's layouts and vents stay the same at any heat, and DK's `roomSetup` passes `rulesConfig: { heat }`. Starting values (*tune by playing*), capacity 12 whole pips: tile weight ¼-pips per cloud tile `[2, 3, 4, 5]` (0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25 a tile), vent puff `[1, 2, 2, 3]` whole pips per vent. Pressure is stored in quarter-pips (integers; the HUD shows whole pips) | The design leaves this open. A multiplier keeps the preview exact and the Lab able to set heat; a schedule would need per-heat vent layouts and would change the clouds themselves, which a player reads worse than a gauge |
| P2 | **The gauge step runs in DK's `afterChemistry` slot**, before layers decay and vents refill. It counts **every cloud tile on the board** (all four cloud kinds, not only one network) plus each vent tile, adds boilermen (P7), and if the total reaches capacity it boils over and resets to 0. It never falls on its own and carries nothing over a boil-over (the surplus is dropped) | Clouds about to expire still count and still storm, so "end the round in the cloud" is what you warn about; counting all tiles is what the player sees as "how much steam is there" |
| P3 | **The boil-over hits each unit standing on a cloud tile for 2 through `dealDamage`**, with a `boilOver` event listing tiles and units, so shields, the AH immunity hook (towel-bearer) and `kill` bursts behave as for any damage. Units are hit in reading order of position; the battle end check runs after the whole step. Its source label is "Boil-over" and it is not a Spark storm (it does not count for the hearing or for storm credit) | Reuses the damage path rather than copying `bursts.ts::storm`; keeps the 500-event cap question a round-end one (FD) |
| P4 | **Forecast is part of the rule's `preview`:** pressure now, next round's fill (from the board as it stands, so it updates in the player's confirm preview), whether it boils over, and the tiles and units it would storm, party members flagged as harm. The HUD is a slim strip along the board's top edge inside the board frame, 28 px high, with whole pips and a forecast tick; it fits a 7×9 board at 375×812 where "beside the board" does not (7 columns of 44 px leave 35 px) | The design says "always visible beside the board". A thin strip keeps 44 px cells; DK-13 owns the generic meter |
| P5 | **Heat per room:** rooms 1–4 base heat 1–4, the heart 4. The effective heat is `max(1, base − cooled)` where `cooled` is the plunge taken before this room (0 or 1). The **heart is never cooled**: the plunge is not offered after room 4, and one taken after room 3 cools room 4 only | The heart is "the hottest room"; the plunge only matters from room 2, where "a real choice by room 3" starts |
| P6 | **The cold plunge is a fixed extra card** in the offers after rooms 1–3 (not rolled): it costs the pick it uses, sets `pendingCool = 1` in trip state on pick, and is gone on a wipe like any loot. **Loot tier follows the room's base heat**, not its cooled heat, so the plunge trades a pick for safety, not for safety plus worse loot | Rolled plunges would make a hot run luck; the cost is already one pick (design) |
| P7 | **The boilerman feeds passively:** while alive and standing orthogonally next to a vent he adds 2 whole pips to the round's fill and the vent beside him puffs even if a state lies on it. His AI walks to the nearest unguarded vent and otherwise bites like a rat. No new intent kind; his effect is shown in the forecast and as a tag on the vent | A passive effect keeps the intent union (RV and DK touch it) unchanged; "priority target" falls out of the forecast number |
| P8 | **The dissolved bather is dissolved while its tile is a cloud tile (derived, never stored).** Dissolved, it ignores single-target hits, pushes, holds and swaps (the hit is cancelled with a visible "passes through"), but **area bursts hurt it** (Storm, Brand storm, the boil-over, a Conduct reaching it is not area). It moves through a connected cloud for free (cloud tiles cost 0, then up to its move over open tiles), attacks only from a non-cloud tile, and so is solid and killable for the player phase after it steps out. **Redefines: none**; this is the reading of "solid only when it steps out" (Q2) | Makes clouds a refuge from bathers and a trap from boil-overs, and makes Storm the answer to them |
| P9 | **The attendant's shove is a foe attack with a push:** `AttackDef.push` (2 tiles, adjacent). Planning picks, among adjacent apprentices and the four headings, the heading whose first obstruction (edge, wall, plate, unit) is nearest; ties go to reading order and `DIRS` order. It resolves through the arrival pipeline (slide and crush as for a player's Shove) and the telegraph shows the line and the crush | "Toward the nearest edge, wall or unit" made deterministic and previewable |
| P10 | **The Steward's two phases:** the stand-in (DK-9 script, no page) carries the real trick as a unit trait through the same harm guard the bather uses; the real Steward (RV API) re-expresses it as a `beforeHarm` reaction. When hit while on a cloud tile by anything but a burst of the cloud, he is not harmed and re-forms on the free connected cloud tile **farthest from the hit** (ties in reading order); with no free tile in his network he takes the hit. Bursts of the cloud (Storm, boil-over) hurt him as they hurt everyone | Settles "re-forms anywhere" into a rule that is testable and that cutting the network defeats |
| P11 | **One harm-guard hook for every plan** (immunity by cause for the towel-bearer, pass-through for the bather, relocation for the Steward, RV's `beforeHarm`): it sits in `dealDamage`/`push`, answers pass, cancel or relocate, and is owned by AH (immunity) and extended by RV-11 (relocation). BH registers its traits; it does not add a second hook | The same wall is being built three times; see requests |
| P12 | **The steam-vent intrusion** is the existing vent terrain in a bath-mosaic skin, two vents on a wider board so clouds overlap, with no gauge and no heat (DK P9). Its Seam loot is the basics | The Seam already has vents; the floor's value is the lore and the overlap lesson, and DK says district-wide rules never come |
| P13 | **The ledger lore is five short lines, one per fight**, each a bather's entry (a name, a number of hours, "stayed") with the owner's name appearing in the heart's entry; shown on the cleared-room screen and kept on the trip page | Matches the canals' lamp-ledger delivery (CN) so a later lore feature can read both |

**Redefines:** none.

## 3. Current implementation and risks

- **Vents and clouds:** `turn.ts::vents` puffs a `cloud` onto a bare vent tile at round end (after `decayStates`); `hazards` runs drift, dusk jars, scheduled, `roundEndChemistry`, `burnGates`, `decayStates`, `coolTiles`, `vents`, `snuffStep`. `queries.ts` has `CLOUDS` (cloud, brandCloud, greaseFog, bank) and `isCloud`; `networks.ts::cloudNet` floods them.
- **Storm:** `bursts.ts::storm` is file-private, per connected network, with a closed `BurstId`; it announces a `burst` event and calls `dealDamage` per unit. The boil-over therefore gets its own event and walks tiles itself (P3).
- **Districts:** `data/districts.ts` holds the five `drawn()` stubs; bathhouses has `material: 'steam'`, `map {75,65}`, line "one fog over the whole house", placeholder hearing `compound:greaseFog` and a `trips ≥ 4` rumour. DK-8 replaces the hearing; DK-17 puts the stub in its own file.
- **Rules content:** Vat, Seep, Steep exist as runes (`data/runes.ts`, `targets.ts`, `resolve.ts`, `mix.ts::steepWide`) and are Lab-only; `data/lines.ts` has **no rows for them** (only `bathSalt:cloud`, `fogJar:unit`), so the footnote audit has real work. `idioms.ts` already detects `steamRoom` (6+ tiles).
- **Foes:** `data/enemies.ts` tokens in use: r b f d e n m M; `ATTACKS` has no pushing attack; AI dispatch is `ai.ts::act` → `foes.ts::foeAct`; `movement.ts::enemyWalk` is BFS-uniform cost. The roster letters D S R N B W T C E and RV's `L` are taken.
- **Risks:** (1) the harm guard (P11) is the critical shared edit: three plans want it and RV-11 sits behind a player gate; (2) a boil-over runs in the round end where deaths can chain (moth bursts) and can end the battle both ways at once, so the event cap and the end-check order need tests; (3) the HUD strip competes with HC's 7×9 board for height at 375×812; (4) Steep spreads clouds and Vat lays four tiles, so Areas builds fill the gauge fast: calibration matters more than anywhere else; (5) the dissolved bather as a refuge-breaker can make a fight unwinnable if a room has no non-cloud tile next to the party's cloud; layouts need a check; (6) district files collide on `src/data/enemies.ts` unless DK's P2 open unions land first.
