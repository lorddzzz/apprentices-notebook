> Design: [district-seam.md](../design/district-seam.md) (all), [district-cartographers.md](../design/district-cartographers.md) §0 (the Seam's truth, the owner's door), [party.md](../design/party.md) §0b and §0d (boards, start points, Seam guarantees), the "Hearing" rows of [ink-makers](../design/district-ink-makers.md), [bathhouses](../design/district-bathhouses.md), [bridges](../design/district-bridges.md), [bell foundry](../design/district-bell-foundry.md) and [city-and-districts.md](../design/city-and-districts.md) §5.3. > Scope: the Marginalia heart, re-laid base rooms, the five hearing events made reachable with owner's hints, the lower Seam framework with its sealed door, and the answering margins.

## 1. Scope and settled rules

**Delivers**
- **The Marginalia** (Seam §0 "Its heart", "The fight"): one tile, lots of HP, the five hands writing a mark, a slick, a cloud, a plate and a spark in a telegraphed cycle; no rival system. **Conduct's guaranteed page on the first heart clear stays** (Seam §1 "Base Seam").
- **Re-laid base rooms** (Seam §1 "Re-laying", party §0b): every layout re-authored at a size chosen for the place, tight and cluttered early, at least four `P` start points, tuned for two (Seam §0 "Role across the game"). This replaces HC-3's mechanical conversion.
- **The five hearing events reachable with the starting basics in at least two base rooms each**, proven by scripted solutions and a bot, each with a `???` hint in the owner's voice (Seam §1 "Hearing setups"). DK-8 swaps the placeholder triggers; SM proves them and writes the hints.
- **The lower Seam framework** (Seam §0–§1): opened by beating the Marginalia; one floor per opened district in opening order, each from that district's `IntrusionSpec`; basics-tier loot; free return; tuned for three; the **sealed door** at the bottom, opened by CG when the Registrar falls (carto §0 "The ending").
- **The answering margins** (Seam §0 "The margins answer"): a second owner's note per page, chosen by mastery or proof, shown only after the Marginalia.

**Leaves out**: the Seam's footnote content pass (FN-A1…C9); recruit preconditions and their guarantees (RC; SM only provides the deep-room slots); every district's own floor content beyond the canals' reference floor (each district build, through SM-0/DK-10); the generic hooks (DK); the ending behind the door (**BL-126**, needs design); door arrival A (RC).

**Settled and kept:** oil-rat, brass sentry and ink familiar stay (Seam §1 "Foes"); free return after any room in the base Seam and on every floor; no optional decision inside a base Seam trip (Seam §0 "The trip"); intrusions never drop their district's runes, methods or solvents (Seam §1 "Intrusion loot"); the Cartographers are not a floor; no scaling for party size (party §0b).

## 3. Current implementation and risks

- **Rooms** (`src/data/encounters.ts`): four encounters, three 6×8 variants each, `D`/`S` placed; depth 1 puts a rat beside a member. Heart "Heart of the Seam" is sentries, rats and one familiar on vents and plates. HC-3 converts these to `P` first.
- **Loot** (`src/data/loot.ts`): `shallow`, `deep`, `heart`; `heart.guaranteed` = Conduct; `rollOffers` in `run.ts` puts it first while missing. Starting runes: Bolt, Strike, Shove, Guard, Ink, Oil, Spark, so Storm and Conduct need the room's own clouds (`@~` vents) and plates (`=`).
- **Hearing** (`src/data/districts.ts`, `src/engine/city.ts` `hearFromBattle`): canals use `burst: fire, atLeast: 3, district: 'seam'` on the largest burst's tile count; the other four use placeholders (`compound:deepMark`, `greaseFog`, `idiom:theWall`, `burst:conduct`) plus rumour `trips`. `hearAfterTrip` marks a heart on any cleared trip (the lower Seam must not).
- **Foes** (`src/data/enemies.ts`, `src/engine/foes.ts`): the familiar's blurb already says "leftover spirit". The lamp-mother is the pattern for a telegraphed special (`SpecialIntent` summon/flare, `resolveSpecial`); DK-9 generalises it into scripted steps.
- **Idioms** (`src/engine/idioms.ts`): `theWall` is matched on the log, with `origin`; `debtCollection` (bound mark) is not the same as the quarter's cross debt.
- **Codex** (`src/data/codex.ts`): every entry has `ownerNote`; `hint` shows under `???` (`CodexEntry.svelte`, `CodexIdiom.svelte`). Proof is in `src/engine/proof.ts`; footnote levels come with FN-1.
- **Risks:** (1) reachability must hold for random start points; a fixed scripted solution proves one draw only, so the bot sweep over all assignments is the real check. (2) The hands' three-tile pattern can stack on the Marginalia's own tile; scenario tests pin self-harm. (3) Re-laying changes the calibration baseline HC-3 records; SM records its own. (4) Floors depend on DK-3/4/10 and on district pieces that do not exist yet; the canal floor is the only proof until IQ/BH/BR/BF land. (5) `Codex.svelte` is also edited by FN-5; SM-13 goes after it.

## 6. Verification

- **Scenarios** (`scenario` skill): each hand; the cycle; mixes with earlier hands; the spark hand's storm, fire and conduct; anchoring against Shove, Pull and Swap; familiars falling; one scripted solution per hearing event per room variant (SM-4).
- **Bot and fuzz** (`tests/sim`): hearing reach over every start-point assignment; base Seam calibration at party 1–3; lower Seam trips over 0–5 fixture floors (never stranded, floors fixed per trip, no district pages in offers, the door state monotone); the 500-event cap respected with the spark hand.
- **Integrity**: `checkRoom` on all Seam layouts, `checkFloor` on every `IntrusionSpec`, every answer's `when` names a subject the page has, five hints for five districts.
- **Phone (375×812, `verify` skill)**: the Marginalia's telegraph and cycle strip; a re-laid 5×6 room with three apprentices; the Seam's margin sheet; the stair, floor labels and door states on the map; Set out's quiet line; an answered codex page. Light and dark. `svelte-autofixer` on every changed component.

## 7. Open questions for the player

1. **Lower Seam as its own trip** (P7, start at floor 1) or as a continuation below the base Seam's heart? *Default:* its own trip, so the tutorial stays four fights and the deep floors are reached directly.
2. **Where the owner's hearing hints live** (P6): the Seam's margin on the map, or under `???` pages in the codex? *Default:* the map, because the hint is about a place you cannot see yet.
3. **The answers' trigger** (P12): per page (proof or that page's mastery), or literally "after you master a school" (a school's runes all at level II)? *Default:* per page.

## 8. Execution notes

- **Critical path:** DK-1 → SM-0 → (DK-9) SM-1 → SM-3 → SM-4 → SM-6 → G1. HC-3 must land before SM-3 (SM overwrites its Seam conversion). The lower Seam waits for DK-17 and DK-10; the margins wait for FN-1.
- **Parallel:** SM-2 and SM-5 beside SM-3/4; Wave 2 can run while G1 is pending; SM-13 (drafting) can start right after SM-0.
- **Later floors:** each district build ships its `IntrusionSpec` (three floor layouts, its pieces) and its pages' answers; SM-8's `checkFloor` is its acceptance test.
- **Size:** 16 tasks and 3 gates: 8 S, 8 M; about an L build, the heart and the reachability proof being the heaviest.
