# UX program review · Home, trip and ending

## Summary verdict

Revise the briefs before dispatch. The painted Study is a plausible composition, but neither its usable editing area nor its unlocked room doors are proved by the listed screenshots.  
The trip can keep its rules and save format. Several acceptance tests would nevertheless reward incorrect behaviour, including invented room clears and a missed homecoming.  
The shared-file order is workable. The pocket, fixture, map-metadata and ending-test handoffs need named owners before the parallel packs start.  
Close names the four required records, but could declare unfinished roadmap work complete.
