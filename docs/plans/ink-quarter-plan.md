# The ink-makers' quarter: the dead-letter office (plan)

> Design: [district-ink-makers.md](../design/district-ink-makers.md); also [rune-design-final.md](../design/rune-design-final.md) §5.2, §6.2, §6.3, §11, [party.md](../design/party.md) §2, [footnotes.md](../design/footnotes.md), [rivals.md](../design/rivals.md). Roadmap: **BL-113**. Prefix **IQ**. Brief: [build-program.md](build-program.md).
> **State: Drafted (not approved).**
> Scope: the Notaries' district as a DK district module: three writ pieces, offers with terms, the postal staff, four rooms and a heart (a scripted stand-in first, the postmaster as a rival later), the return-to-sender gate, the cross-apprentice hearing's data, letters to the owner, the junior notary's landmark room, its lower-Seam floor, its footnote batch, Bench parity and bot calibration.

## 1. Scope and settled rules

**Delivers** (design cited, not copied): writs as board pieces (§0 "Board rule", "Writ kinds"); offers with terms, their three stakes, and "a signed term blocks going home" (§0 "Trip shape" to "Going home"); the rare treasures only with a term (Bind, Wax, Sand) and Sentence, Levy, Tally in ordinary offers; the Sorter, the Sealer and the dead letter; four rooms and the heart, writs by room as in §1; the postmaster's clock contract; the hearing's data and the returned-letter moment; the "return to sender" threshold; letters to the owner; the junior notary's landmark room; the lower-Seam writ floor; the Contracts footnote batch (Sentence, Levy, Tally, Wax, Sand, the postmaster's note); Bench and Lab parity; bot calibration of rooms and term numbers.

**Settled and kept:** the three writ kinds and no "opened by a death"; no debt carried into a later offer; terms always visible and refusable; terms never beyond a normal room's difficulty band; no landings (the terms decide); the heart is a return point; threshold rooms are free and retryable; Debt Collection is already an engine idiom (`engine/idioms.ts`).

**Leaves out** (owner): the framework (DK), the arrival pipeline (FD), start points and sizes (HC), the recruit offer and its preconditions (RC), the notary's Swap and her Tally habit (AH), rival engine and the page/Bench trial machinery (RV), the lower-Seam framework (SM), footnote levels and slots (FN), hearing-kind code (`crossDebt` is DK-8; the Seam rooms where it is reachable are SM's).

## 2. Proposals

| # | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Writ pieces** are DK pieces: `{writ:return}`, `{writ:order}`, and a pair `{writ:forward:a}` + `{address:a}` (the letter pairs a source stamp with an addressee tile). Walkable, never block shots, permanent; not movable by Draw or Erase here (CG decides later). All three hooks are exempt only for anchored units: **fliers and dead letters are read too** | A stamp is a rule, not a surface; "return to sender" must work on a paper foe |
| P2 | **Return to sender** fires when a unit **lands** on the stamp by any cause except `return` and `restore` (walk, push, slide, pull, hop, swap, drift, carry). It goes to its **start tile of the round**: its position at the end of the last dusk (a writs rule snapshot taken in DK's `late` slot, plus the battle's first positions); a unit with no snapshot stays. If that tile is taken, the **nearest free standable tile** (Manhattan, then reading order). Returning does not trigger writs (no chains). Foes' planners never choose to end a move on this stamp; a member's walk preview warns and a landing spends the member's move | Readable, no loops, never a dead end. For a foe it means "your advance this round is undone"; for a member it is a trap tile with a visible price |
| P3 | **Forwarding.** While a unit stands on the stamp, a hit it would take (damage from any source, or a mark) lands on the **unit on the address tile** instead; an empty address means it lands normally. Once per stamp per round; a forwarded hit is not forwarded again; the forwarded hit is attributed to the original origin. Needs a pre-harm interception from DK (Request D1) | "Passes damage or a mark ... to a unit elsewhere" made exact, with the smallest set of limits |
| P4 | **Standing order** marks a plain Ink mark (no owner) on each unit standing on it at round end (DK `late` slot, after decay), **only if the unit has no mark** (it never overwrites a bound mark) | Leaves Bind intact; "ends a turn" for both sides is the one moment dusk |
| P5 | **A term is reward + stake + condition, and settles when the next room is won** (a term signed in room 4 settles in the heart). Open terms block return through DK-5a's hook with the reason shown; at most one is open at a time; the heart's loot has none, so a party can never be stranded. A term is not breakable on purpose (Q1). A wipe loses everything as usual | The design's "paid or broken" needs a deadline; "the next room" is the only one all three stakes share |
| P6 | **Stakes** (`TermDef`, data): **reward** (the page is held and removed if the condition fails); **resources** (on failure, 1-2 HP off each of the party, or 1 charge each, or 2 ink from the trip's ink, never below revive HP); **next room** (always applied: a mark on one apprentice, one extra dead letter, or a seal on one apprentice's start tile; the apprentice is picked from the trip seed). Conditions: `winRoom`, `withinRounds(N)`, `noneDowned` (DK's `Goal` language plus one predicate). A term may also grant **an extra pick** (the design's "stake 2 ink for a second page") | Matches the three examples in §1; every number is data and calibrated by the bot (IQ-20) |
| P7 | **Offer placement.** A termed offer is an **extra card** beside the normal three, never replacing one, at most one per room, always showing reward, condition and penalty. Room 1 always carries a low-stakes sampler term. **Bind's termed page is guaranteed in room 2 while the household lacks it** (with a gentle condition); Wax and Sand are termed offers in rooms 2-4. Sentence, Levy, Tally are ordinary weighted offers; **Tally is guaranteed in rooms 1 and 2 while lacking** (RC's precondition rule for the notary). **Lift's page is a guaranteed ordinary offer in the heart's loot** (the design leaves it open): terms cannot exist at the heart, it matches Conduct, Repeat and Margin, and it gives the postmaster a reward the Bind term does not | Bind stays optional and retryable; no soft-lock for the notary; the heart pays something |
| P8 | **The postal staff** (numbers *tune by playing*, in `src/data/`): **Sorter** (4 HP, move 2; telegraphs "forward the mark on X to Y" by moving a mark from a foe or itself onto an apprentice, fizzles if the mark is gone, else a 1-damage adjacent file); **Sealer** (5 HP, move 1; telegraphs a tile within 3, a wax seal lands there when the action resolves, holds whoever stands on it, lasts 2 rounds, melts in fire, vanishes when the Sealer dies); **dead letter** (1 HP, move 2, flies, addressed to the nearest apprentice at planning; bursts for 2 on delivery; dying or bursting leaves an ink **blot** on its tile, so it feeds Tally) | One role and one telegraph each (§0 "Foes"); the blot keeps the staff on theme |
| P9 | **The clock writ.** The postmaster serves it by placing two things: **his seal** (an anchored 3-HP inert unit, "break his seal") and **his stamp** (a tile that answers the writ when any apprentice lands on it, with no mark). Deadline = serve round + 3. **Answered** (seal dies, or stamp landed on): the writ lifts and he **loses his next action**. **Unanswered at dusk of the deadline: every mark on the board collects** (3 to its bearer, mark spent, never released), on **both sides**, so marking him or his staff is a counter. He serves again 2 rounds later. One engine module serves the stand-in and the rival | "Telegraphed and readable; a term you can meet or break" (§0 "Heart"); "collects" needed an owner |
| P10 | **Heart in two steps.** Ship with a scripted stand-in (DK-9): a cycle of serve the writ, pen strike, seal. It clears the district, awards **no page**, is flagged `standIn`. Once RV's authoring API exists the postmaster replaces it in data (IQ-22). Default kit for the Lab: *Serve notice* `Bolt > Ink > Sentence` (marks, then herds 2 tiles onto a stamp or edge), *Collect* `Splash > Levy` (shields him per mark), routine "mark, then herd, then levy when 2+ marks are near him". His **page recovers *Serve notice***; his **note is on Sentence, level II** (FN's Q3 default) | The design allows a stand-in; the five-page hearing (CG) needs real hearts, so the swap must be cheap |
| P11 | **Threshold "return to sender"**: a 6x8 board, no foes, 4+ `P`, an inert undelivered letter (a non-flying `idle` unit) on its desk tile, a stamp across the room, plates or a wall so one Bolt can't reach, solvable with `Bolt > Shove` from every start point. Goal: the **letter is sent back** (a unit-keyed event goal, Request D3), so an apprentice stepping on the stamp doesn't open the door | Solvable by anyone; the keyed goal is the only way to keep it honest |
| P12 | **Lower-Seam floor:** one `IntrusionSpec` with the **return stamp** (two tiles per room), three layouts, Seam foes, basics loot, tuned for three. It brings the piece and its snapshot rule (piece-scoped, DK P9), never the district | Most readable writ; teaches pushing without terms |
| P13 | **Letters to the owner** are five **found papers** (rooms 1-4 and the postmaster's desk, where the expulsion notice sits). Winning a room shows its letter in the loot screen, free (no pick), once per profile, filed in a Codex section "Dead letters". No generic lore system exists (BL-41); IQ builds the smallest one (`Profile.papers`, a registry by id) for the other districts to reuse (Request O2) | Pieces "found across the rooms" without making them loot |
| P14 | **Hearing data and the returned letter.** IQ supplies the `crossDebt` entry (owner voice) and the home moment: a letter stamped RETURNED TO SENDER lands in the attic before the district is drawn in | DK-8 owns the kind; SM proves reachability; the copy is the district's |
| P15 | **Layout** per DK P1: `src/data/districts/inkQuarter.ts` plus `inkQuarter/` (rooms, loot, terms, foes, papers, threshold, floor), `src/engine/districts/inkQuarter.ts` plus `inkQuarter/` (writs, terms, clock, foes), `src/ui/districts/inkQuarter/`, `src/data/footnotes/inkQuarter.ts`. Loot tables are `inkQuarter:*` | Parallel builds touch disjoint files |

**Redefines: none.** P5-P7 and P9-P10 fill gaps the design left ("how terms are saved", Lift, "collects", the stand-in).

Rooms (placeholder names; writs by room as in the design §1): 1 *The sorting hall* (return; dead letters), 2 *The blotting room* (adds order; Sorter), 3 *The tube hall* (adds forward; Sealer; the notary's desk), 4 *The ledger stacks* (all three; Sealer, Sorter, letters), heart *The postmaster's counter* (his clock; Sealer, letters). Boards sized and start-pointed per HC's `checkRoom`; three layouts per depth.

## 3. Current implementation and risks

- `src/data/districts.ts`: `inkQuarter` is a `drawn()` stub (no rooms, `gate: 'drawing'`, placeholder hearing `compound:deepMark` and `trips >= 3`); DK-8 and IQ-8 replace it. `PLAYABLE_DISTRICTS` is derived by DK.
- `engine/run.ts`: `rollOffers` takes one table with one `guaranteed` spec and a closed `LootItem` union; `finishEncounter` rolls offers and `pickLoot` banks them; `returnHome` is guarded by `canReturn`. Terms need DK-5a/5b/7 (return block, trip state, `afterRoom`, offer kinds, extra picks).
- `engine/marks.ts`: `applyMark` replaces a unit's mark (spent, not released); `Mark.wax` and `turnBack` (Sand) already exist, so the solvents' effects are built. Bind and Lift exist in `engine/inventions.ts`.
- Foes: `EnemyAi` is a closed union dispatched in `ai.ts`/`foes.ts`; `Intent` is switched on in `view.ts`, `overlay.ts`, `describe.ts`. Every district foe would touch all of them (Request D2).
- `turn.ts` `hazards` is a fixed list; DK-3 gives the three slots IQ uses. Damage has no pieces' interception point (Request D1).
- Tests: `tests/sim/calibration.test.ts` calibrates the Seam only; `bot.ts` has no notion of offers with stakes.
- **Risks:** (1) forwarding intercepts damage and marks, both shared with every district; (2) return/arrival depends on FD-3 landing first; (3) a term that strands or kills is the worst failure, so fairness is tested by data (IQ-4, IQ-20); (4) the heart's stand-in must not become the final heart by neglect (IQ-22 is on the critical path of CG); (5) Lab playtest numbers for the postmaster may change the clock.

## 6. Verification

- **Scenarios** (`scenario` skill): each writ from each cause and trait; occupied start tile and no chain; forwarding (damage, mark, empty, once per round); standing order skips marked units; seal dodge, melt and death; the Sorter and dead letter lines; the four clock outcomes; term sign, pay, break, wipe, reload and the return block; the threshold solved from every start point and not by stepping on the stamp; the lower floor; hearing with starting basics; recruit gating; Debt Collection through quarter content.

- **Phone checks** (375x812, `verify` skill): each stamp and its preview, a termed card, the mid-room banner, the refused Crossroads, the clock heart, the threshold, the papers screen, the Bench with Bind and Lift; light and dark. Svelte autofixer on each component.
- `pnpm check` on every task.

## 7. Open questions for the player

1. **Break a term on purpose?** Default no: a signed term binds you through the next room (P5). Allowing a forfeit-and-go-home would remove the "wipe risk" the design wants.
2. **Standing order marks apprentices too** (as designed). Is a marked apprentice acceptable when the clock can collect it? Default yes: it is the clock's fuel and the Sorter's.
3. **The postmaster's note on Sentence at level II** (FN's Q3 default), or Tally at level I?
