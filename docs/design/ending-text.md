| 1 | You found the handle. I wondered which of you would. Sit down, the stool is older than the guild and it has held worse than you. I have been writing to you for a long time. It is good to do it across a desk instead of across a join. | The margins answering |
| 2 | This is the join: the place where the guild's drawings of the city meet and do not agree. They only ever anchored whole streets, so nobody hung a lamp here or cast a bell for it. | 4 | When it began to tear I went in and held it. I tried ink first. The hands that climbed your pages and the Marginalia were mine once: attempts that stayed after I stopped needing them.
